2026-2027 All Star Information

Come grow with us. Bloom All Star Cheer is a team commitment that includes practices, skill development, routine training, performances, and competitions.

Welcome to Bloom All Star Cheer

Thank you for your interest in the Bloom Cheer & Tumbling All Star Cheer program. We are excited to offer athletes a positive, structured, and exciting opportunity to grow in confidence, strength, skill, teamwork, and performance.

All Star Cheer is more than a weekly class. Our goal is to help athletes become strong cheerleaders while also learning responsibility, discipline, sportsmanship, and what it means to be part of a team.

Season Commitment

The information below explains team placement, financial expectations, practice requirements, attendance, parent responsibilities, competition plans, and athlete expectations. Families should review each section carefully before committing to the season.

Team Placement

Evaluation and placement process

Evaluations help coaches place athletes on the team where they will be most successful and safe.

Evaluation Skills

Athletes may be evaluated on the following areas:

  • Jumps, motions, dance, and performance
  • Standing tumbling and running tumbling
  • Stunting ability, flexibility, and strength
  • Learning pace, coachability, attention to detail, attitude, and work ethic

Evaluations are not meant to be stressful. Athletes will warm up, practice, and show the skills they are comfortable performing.

Team Placement

All athletes will be placed on a team. Placement is based on age, skill level, safety, and what will help each athlete and team be successful.

All Star Cheer is not only about tumbling. Stunting, jumps, motions, performance, teamwork, and routine execution are also important parts of team placement.

Coaches make final placement decisions based on what is best for the athlete and the team.

Season Expectations

All Star Cheer is a team sport. Every athlete has an important role in the routine, and regular attendance is necessary for the team to be successful.

Athletes are expected to attend all scheduled practices, performances, and competitions. Missing practice affects the athlete, stunt groups, formations, timing, and the entire routine.

Practices may include warm-up and stretching, stunts, tumbling, jumps, motions, dance, conditioning, routine sections, full-out routine practice, and routine cleaning. Additional practices may be added closer to showcases or competitions if needed.

Each All Star athlete will have a Bloom Athlete Account. This account is separate from monthly tuition and is used for season-related expenses. Monthly gym membership tuition is completely separate from the Bloom Athlete Account.

The Bloom Athlete Account may cover uniforms, bows, choreography, music, competition fees, coaches’ competition fees, showcase fees, and team-related expenses. Families may pay into the account through scheduled payments, fundraising credits, or personal deposits.

Fundraising profits can be applied to the athlete’s account to help reduce out-of-pocket costs.

All members must complete a Bloom Athlete Account Payment Intent Form as part of the online commitment forms. Families may choose to fully participate in fundraising, partially participate, or pay all season expenses out-of-pocket.

All members are required to keep a debit or credit card on file for their Bloom Athlete Account. This card will only be charged if a scheduled payment is not fully covered by fundraising credits or personal deposits by the due date.

For example, if a $300 payment is due on August 15 and an athlete has earned $100 through fundraising, only the remaining $200 is due. If that balance is not paid by the due date, the card on file will be automatically charged for the remaining amount, plus a card processing fee.

Families will receive access to a Google account link showing deposits and deductions. It is the family’s responsibility to review this regularly and keep the account current.

If the card on file is inactive or declined, and the required payment has not been made by the scheduled due date, the athlete will not be allowed to participate in practice or be choreographed into the routine. If payments remain past due after choreography, the athlete may be replaced with no refund issued.

Tuition and the Bloom Athlete Account may not include cheer shoes, makeup, extra bows, optional spirit wear, warm-ups, backpacks, private lessons, extra clinics, travel expenses, hotel expenses, food at competitions, spectator admission fees, or optional end-of-season events.

Fundraisers are optional and can help families offset athlete expenses throughout the season. Money earned through approved fundraisers will be applied directly to the athlete’s Bloom Athlete Account and may be used toward eligible cheer-related expenses.

Families may fully participate, partially participate, or opt out. Any remaining balance not covered through fundraising is the responsibility of the parent or guardian and must be paid by the scheduled due dates.

Funds raised are non-refundable and remain connected to the athlete’s account for approved program expenses. Fundraising does not replace payment deadlines unless the athlete has earned enough funds in the Bloom Athlete Account before the payment due date.

Competitions and Communication

Bloom All Star Cheer teams may participate in local performances, showcases, and competitions. Competition schedules will depend on team placement, readiness, athlete numbers, and the overall season plan.

Possible events may include a family and friends showcase, local community performances, local competitions, regional competitions, and optional end-of-season events.

Competition season will typically take place during the second half of the season. Families will receive a tentative schedule once teams are formed and events are selected.

All athletes participating on a Bloom All Star competitive team will be required to join the U.S. All Star Federation, also known as USASF. USASF provides structure, safety standards, age verification, rules, and competition guidelines for All Star cheer and dance.

USASF membership is separate from Bloom Cheer & Tumbling fees and must be completed by each athlete or parent through the USASF membership system. Athlete memberships are renewed each season and are required for athletes to compete at USASF-sanctioned events.

The current athlete membership fee listed by USASF is $49 per season, with the membership term running June 1 through May 31. Athletes who are 18 or older during the membership season may have additional USASF requirements, including background screening and abuse prevention training.

Communication is an important part of a successful season. Parents are responsible for staying informed about practices, payments, schedules, competitions, uniform information, and team updates.

Bloom Cheer & Tumbling may communicate through email, text message, Band app or team app, website, social media, and parent meetings. Parents are expected to check communication regularly and respond when needed.

Athlete and Family Expectations

Athletes should arrive ready to practice with hair pulled up and out of the face, proper practice wear, cheer shoes or approved athletic shoes, no jewelry, trimmed nails, a water bottle, and a positive attitude.

Once team practice wear is assigned, athletes will be expected to wear the correct outfit to practice.

Sportsmanship and team unity are extremely important at Bloom Cheer & Tumbling. Athletes and parents are expected to represent the gym in a positive way at all times.

Athletes and families should not speak negatively about other athletes, coaches, parents, teams, other gyms, scores, placements, or competition results. This includes comments made in person, by text, or on social media.

Athletes are expected to follow all gym rules during practices, performances, competitions, and team events.

General expectations include being respectful to coaches, teammates, parents, and staff; arriving on time and ready to work; wearing no jewelry during practice or competition; no gum; keeping cell phones put away during practice; practicing only when a coach is present; using appropriate language; showing good sportsmanship; following uniform and appearance expectations; respecting the facility and equipment; and maintaining a positive attitude.

If an athlete quits or is removed from the program by Bloom Cheer & Tumbling, no refund will be issued. This includes prepaid Bloom Athlete Account amounts and fundraised money.

Tuition payment will continue to be drafted June 2026-April 2027 regardless of departure, and families will be required to pay an in-full, up-front cancellation fee of $550. Bloom Cheer & Tumbling must be able to fund a replacement athlete because team numbers are chosen based on each athlete having a position.

By joining Bloom All Star Cheer, athletes and families are committing to the team, the season, and the expectations listed in this packet. All Star Cheer requires dedication, teamwork, consistency, and a positive attitude.

Our goal is to provide athletes with a fun, structured, and encouraging environment where they can grow as cheerleaders and as individuals. Thank you for being part of the Bloom Cheer & Tumbling family. Come grow with us!
