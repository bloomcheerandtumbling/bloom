Simple pricing for growing athletes

Monthly tuition is based on class level. A $40 registration fee applies when enrolling.

Beginner Tumbling

$65 / month

Foundational tumbling instruction for athletes building confidence, coordination, and safe technique.

  • Beginner skills
  • Safe progressions
  • Confidence-building coaching

Intermediate Tumbling

$85 / month

Skill-focused training for athletes ready to build strength, form, and tumbling connections.

  • Walkovers and handspring drills
  • Technique and control
  • Progressive skill development

Advanced Tumbling

$100 / month

Higher-level tumbling instruction for athletes working on power, technique, and advanced skills.

  • Advanced skill progressions
  • Power and body control

  • End-of-session family performance

  • Team commitment and growth

  • Team commitment and growth
