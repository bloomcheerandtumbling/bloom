How to Register for Open Gym & All Star Evaluation

To register your child for Open Gym and the All Star Evaluation, please follow these steps carefully.

  1. Follow the registration link.
  2. Begin by registering for All Star and Open Gyms and Evaluations first. This is required to access the event registration system.
  3. Connect to the Parent Portal.
  4. From the menu, select Activities, then click Find Events.
  5. Scroll through the list until you find the Open Gym that fits your child’s age group.
  6. Please note: there are two time slots per age group. Choose the one that works best for your family. If neither time slot works for you, please contact us at coach@bloomcheerandtumbling.com for assistance.
  7. Select the Open Gym you would like, select your child’s name, then select add.
  8. Select Continue Shopping.
  9. Next, scroll to find the All Star Evaluation date that matches your child’s age and add that event as well.
  10. Once you have selected at least one Open Gym AND the All Star Evaluation, proceed to checkout to complete your child’s enrollment in the evaluation process.

If you need help at any point during registration, please reach out. We are happy to assist.
