All-Star Evaluations for Placement

August 28 • 5:00–6:30 p.m.

Evaluation fee: $40 What to wear: T-shirt, athletic shorts, and tennis shoes

Athletes will work with our coaches as we look at their current skills and determine the best team placement for them. All skill levels are welcome — whether you’re brand new to cheer or already have experience! During evaluations, athletes will have the opportunity to show us their jumps, motions, tumbling, and other cheer skills. Don’t worry if you haven’t learned everything yet — we’re looking for athletes who are excited to learn, grow, work hard, and be part of a team.
